The maths, assumptions & sources

Spacecraft: Δv = Isp × g₀ × ln(m₀ / mfinal). Reserved fuel stays onboard. Mass flow = thrust / (Isp × g₀). Duration = consumed mass / mass flow. One stage and engine throughout.

Lunar transfer: v² = μ(2/r − 1/a). The transfer ellipse connects source apoapsis to the mean surface or target periapsis. Coast time = π√(a³/μ). Landing budget cancels the entire periapsis velocity relative to the rotating surface. Terminal stopping is an ideal impulse; finite powered descent is not integrated.

Geometry: Circular orbits depart at any phase; eccentric orbits only at apoapsis. The fixed orbital plane is initialized relative to the body at your epoch. The Moon rotates uniformly in 27.3217 days. No libration, precession, mascons, third-body gravity, terrain or divert. A 30-second coarse search with refined minima and boundaries finds pin-radius crossings. Tracks are samples, not a continuous reachable-area solution.

Earth leg: Ideal perigee departure into a coplanar ellipse ending at 384,400 km geocentric radius, then tangential relative-speed capture. Encounter phase and B-plane targeting are not solved. Capture is assumed at the most recent perilune before the lunar orbit epoch, inferred from mean anomaly. Earth departure time is a backward estimate from that capture, not a launch window.

Terrain views: NASA’s downloadable topographic GLB supplies the colour map. Its surface is remeshed from the linked physical LOLA elevation data to remove presentation exaggeration and repair documented pole artifacts. The simplified global mesh is an overview; narrow features can differ substantially from the finer regional view. The global polar cap averages the adjacent latitude row. The regional view uses the original 16-pixel-per-degree elevation grid, tiled without resampling. It uses a curved, local tangent view; display vertices are bilinearly interpolated. Height values are metres relative to a 1,737.4 km radius. Terrain loading does not make the trajectory or landing-safety checks terrain-aware.

Limits: Positive fuel margin and thrust/weight above one at the landing mass are screening checks, not proof of landing feasibility. For crewed missions, felt engine acceleration is checked against NASA sustained +Gz limits (NASA/TM-20205008196); crew status does not change delta-v, and life support and abort options are not assessed. Moon-surface launch is not included. Dates are UTC; scenario states are not measured spacecraft ephemerides.
