Data provenance & how to read this explorer

Sun and Earth apparent azimuth/elevation come from JPL Horizons, queried for each coordinate on the Moon (coord@301), in degrees clockwise from north. Values are cached hourly for November 2026, not live telemetry. At exact cached hours these samples are used unchanged. Other times use the same approximate ephemeris as Surface & space, clearly labeled calculated estimates. Playback continues without a fixed date cutoff; it does not fetch additional JPL data. Accuracy outside the cached comparison period is unvalidated.

The default horizon is the local 0° reference plane. The panorama foreground is decorative, not mapped terrain. The illustrative ridge is synthetic. Import a measured horizon for the selected coordinate to compare body-centre elevation against linearly interpolated horizon elevation. Profile provenance and accuracy remain the uploader’s responsibility. Imported profiles stay in this browser session and are not uploaded.

Visibility uses body centres, not finite disks. Sampling can miss short events. Reference-plane durations are counts of hourly samples and longest runs are clipped to the month. Sun visibility does not establish solar power; Earth visibility does not establish a communications link. Actual terrain visibility is unknown without an appropriate measured profile.
